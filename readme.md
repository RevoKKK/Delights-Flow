# Delights Flow — User Guide

**Delights Flow** is a private, real-time **Money Tracker**, **Multi-Shop Manager**, and **Customer Receipt Generator** designed for individuals, couples, and small online sellers.

It keeps your real-world wallets (Cash, Bank, KPay, CBPay, etc.) synced across Android, iOS, and Desktop—even when you go offline—while keeping personal spending and online shop performance clearly organized in one unified app.

## Quick Start — How to Start Using Delights Flow

Follow these 5 simple steps to get started in under 2 minutes:

1. **Open the App:** Open **[Delights Flow](https://revokkk.github.io/Delights-Flow/)** in **Chrome** (Android/Windows/macOS), **Safari** (iPhone/iPad), or **Edge**.
2. **Install to Your Home Screen *(Optional, Recommended)*:** Add Delights Flow to your home screen (see Section 1) so it opens full-screen like a native app and works offline.
3. **Sign In:** Tap **Continue with Google** or sign in with **Email & Password** (**Create Account** for first-time users).
4. **Create or Join a Ledger:**
   * **Starting fresh?** Choose **Create New Ledger**, pick your ledger name, currency (e.g., `Ks`), and a 4+ character Partner Join PIN.
   * **Sharing with a partner?** Choose **Join Partner's** and enter the **Workspace ID** and **Join PIN** they shared with you.
5. **Set Your Wallets & Start Tracking:** Tap **Manage Wallets** on **Overview** to add your real-world accounts (Cash, Bank, KPay, CBPay, etc.) and enter each account's current **Opening Balance**. Tap **+** anytime to record your first entry, or switch to **Shop** to start recording sales and receipts.

## 2. Sign In & Set Up Your Ledger

### Sign In
* Tap **Continue with Google** for instant sign-in, or use **Email & Password** (**Sign In** / **Create Account**).
* If you forget your email password, tap **Forgot password?** on the Sign-In screen.

### Create Your Own Private Ledger
1. On the **Set Up Your Ledger** screen, choose **Create New Ledger**.
2. Enter your **Ledger Name** (e.g., *My Ledger* or *Home & Shop*), **Currency** (e.g., `Ks`), and a **Partner Join PIN** (at least 4 characters).
3. Tap **Create Private Ledger**. Every new ledger starts clean with **Cash** and **Bank** wallets.

### Share a Ledger with a Partner (2-Person Live Sync)
1. **Person 1:** Open **Settings** (sliders icon) → scroll to **Private Workspace & Partner Invite** → copy both the **Workspace ID** and **Join PIN** and send them privately to your partner.
2. **Person 2:** Sign in with their own Google or Email account → select **Join Partner's** → enter the **Workspace ID** and **Join PIN** → tap **Join Shared Ledger**.
3. Both devices now see live updates across all wallets, personal entries, shops, and receipts.

> You can customize **Person 1** and **Person 2** names and tag colors, switch between multiple ledgers, or create/join another ledger anytime inside **Settings**.

## 3. Using the Money Tracker

Navigate using the bottom bar on mobile (**Overview · Shop · (+) · Analytics · History**) or the left sidebar on desktop. The mobile **+** button is context-aware—indigo for recording Money entries and emerald when you are in Shop views.

### Manage Wallets & Opening Balances
1. On **Overview**, tap **Manage Wallets**.
2. Tap **+ Add Wallet** to add bank accounts or mobile wallets (e.g., *KBZ Bank*, *KPay*, *CBPay*).
3. Customize each wallet's **Name**, **Type** (*Cash*, *Bank*, *Mobile Pay*, *Card / Pay*), **Color** (18 options), and **Opening Balance** (supports positive or negative balances), then tap **Save Wallets**.

### Record Expense, Income, or Transfer
1. Tap **+** (mobile on Overview/Money views) or **+ New Money Entry** (desktop).
2. Choose **Expense**, **Income**, or **Transfer**.
3. Enter the amount (use quick chips like `+1,000` to `+100,000` for faster entry).
4. Pick the wallet(s) and category (tap **+ Custom** to add your own category), set the **Date**, select the **Owner** (*Person 1*, *Person 2*, or *Shared*), and add an optional note.
5. Tap **Save Entry**. Wallet balances update immediately.
   * *Transfers move money between two wallets without inflating your monthly Income or Expense totals.*

### 1-Tap Quick Templates
* Save frequent entries (like daily coffee, commute, or monthly bills) by tapping **+ Manage** in the **Quick Templates** section—or by checking **Also save as 1-tap Quick Template** when adding a new entry.
* Tap any template card on **Overview** to log it instantly.

### Switch Active User
* Tap the colored user pill in the top header (mobile) or the switcher at the bottom of the sidebar (desktop) to switch quickly between **Person 1** and **Person 2**.

### Browse Wallet History & Calendar View
* Open **History** and select the **Wallet** sub-tab.
* **List vs. Calendar:** Switch between date-grouped **List** view and **Calendar** view (shows daily income/expense totals on each day; tap any date to inspect or add entries for that day).
* **Filters:** Search by keyword or filter by **Month**, **Wallet**, **Type**, **Owner**, or **Scope** (*Personal + Shop*, *Personal Only*, *Shop Only*).
* **Quick Wallet Filter:** Tap any wallet card on **Overview** to jump straight to transactions for that wallet; tapping **History** in the navigation bar resets back to **All Wallets**.
* **Shop Entries in Wallet History:** Tap any entry marked **🛍️ Shop** to open its receipt or edit screen directly.

### View Money Analytics
* Open **Analytics** and select **Money Analytics** to inspect **Period Income**, **Period Expense**, **Net Savings**, and **Savings Rate**, along with charts for **Expenses by Category**, **6-Month Income vs. Expense Trend**, **Live Wallet Balances**, and **Owner Breakdown**.
* Use the scope selector at the top to switch between **Personal + Shop**, **Personal Only**, and **Shop Only**.

## 4. Using Multi-Shop & Customer Receipts

Tap **Shop** in the navigation bar to open your shop dashboard. Tap **Overview** anytime to return to your personal Money Tracker.

### Set Up & Manage Multiple Shops
1. On **Shop**, tap **Manage Shops**.
2. New ledgers start with **Shop 1**—rename it or tap **+ Add Shop** to run multiple shops separately.
3. For each shop, you can customize:
   * **Shop Name** & **Color** (18 color options)
   * **Shop Logo** (tap the shop icon or **📷 Upload Logo** — appears automatically on receipts)
   * **Starting Capital** (tracked toward your break-even / capital recovery progress)
   * **Receipt Footer** (thank-you note or shop policy shown at the bottom of receipts)
4. Tap **Save Shops**.

### Record a Shop Sale (Full Paid, Full COD, or Partial)
1. Tap **+** (mobile while on Shop, Shop History, or Shop Analytics) or **+ Record Shop Sale** (desktop sidebar).
2. Select the **Shop**, enter optional **Customer Name** and **Phone**, and add one or more items (**Item name**, **Qty**, **Price**). Add **Delivery Fee** or **Discount** if needed.
3. Choose how the customer is paying under **Payment (Paid / COD)**:
   * **Full Paid:** The full amount is added to your selected wallet right away.
   * **Full COD:** Nothing is added to your wallet yet; the full amount is tracked under **Pending COD**.
   * **Partial Paid + COD:** Enter the **Paid Now** deposit (added to your wallet immediately) and the remaining **COD Amount** stays pending until delivery payment arrives.
4. Choose the deposit wallet, check **Open Customer Receipt immediately after saving** if desired, and tap **Save Sale**.

### Mark COD Orders as Paid
1. Find any pending COD order in **Recent Shop Activity**, **Shop History**, or by tapping the **⏳ Pending COD** bar on Shop Home.
2. Tap **✓ Mark COD Paid**.
3. Select the wallet where the delivery cash/transfer was received and the received date, then tap **✓ Confirm Paid**. Your wallet balance, daily/monthly totals, and calendar views update automatically on the received date.

### Generate & Share Customer Receipts
* Tap **🧾 Receipt** on any sale (or let it open right after saving a sale).
* Each receipt displays that shop's **Name**, **Logo**, **Receipt No.**, **Date**, **Customer details**, itemized list, **Total**, **Paid / COD status**, and **Footer**.
* Choose how to send it to your customer:
  * **↗ Share PNG:** Share the receipt image directly to Messenger, Viber, or other apps.
  * **↓ Download PNG:** Save the receipt image to your device.
  * **📋 Copy Text Receipt:** Copy a clean text slip to paste straight into chat.

### Record Shop Capital & Costs
1. Open the Shop entry modal (via **+** on mobile or **+ Record Shop Sale** on desktop) and switch to the **📦 Capital / Cost** tab—or tap **📦 + Add Cost** on any shop card inside **Manage Shops**.
2. Select the **Shop** (or *General Shop*), choose a **Cost Type** (*Stock / Capital*, *Packaging*, *Delivery*, *Ads / Boost*, *Other*), enter the amount, and pick the wallet you paid from.
3. Saving automatically deducts the amount from your wallet and updates your shop's **Total Capital**, **Net Profit / To Break Even**, and **Capital Recovery %**.

### Shop History, Calendar & Shop Analytics
* Tap any **Shop Card** on Shop Home to view that shop's history, or open **History → 🛍️ Shop** to switch between **List** and **Calendar** views, filter by shop/month/**Pending COD**, and export a **Shop CSV**.
* Open **Analytics → 🛍️ Shop Analytics** to filter by **All Shops** or an individual shop and inspect **Daily/Monthly Sales & Capital Trends**, **Sales vs. Capital by Shop**, **Average Order Value**, and **Capital & Cost Breakdown**.

## 5. Privacy, Backups & Daily Tips

* **Privacy Mode:** Tap the **eye icon** inside the **Total Net Balance** card on **Overview** to hide or show your total balance, monthly hero figures, and live wallet balances (`••••••`) when using the app in public.
* **Dark & Light Mode:** Tap the **moon/sun icon** in the top bar (mobile) or sidebar (desktop) to switch themes anytime.
* **Offline Ready & Auto-Updates:** Once opened, Delights Flow loads instantly even without internet. Entries recorded offline save immediately without freezing and sync automatically once you reconnect. App updates refresh cleanly in the background and prompt you when ready.
* **Export CSV:** Export filtered Wallet or Shop records to Excel-friendly CSV files from **History**.
* **Backup & Restore (JSON):** Periodically save a full backup of your wallets, shops, templates, and transactions via **Settings → Download Backup**, and restore anytime using **Restore JSON**.
* **Smart Navigation & Back Button:** On mobile, pressing Back closes open popups first, returns to **Overview** from other tabs, and displays an **Exit Delights Flow?** prompt on Overview—press Back a second time to exit natively, or tap **Stay in App** to continue. On desktop, press **Escape** to close open popups.
